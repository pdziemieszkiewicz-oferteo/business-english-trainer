Ride mode

The app speaks one sentence, waits for your repetitions, optionally beeps between repetition windows, and moves to the next sentence automatically.

Headset controls: Play/Pause, Previous and Next are registered through the browser Media Session API when supported. Browser/headset support varies.

Offline: the PWA shell and imported lesson are stored locally. TTS can work offline when the selected English voice is installed on your phone. Keep the screen awake for the most reliable ride session.
